Good to know

Is RTC really free?

Yes. Plan building, custom muscle splits, all 171 programmes, meal planning, food tracking, barcode scanning, badges and progress tracking are free. RTC Pro adds extra tools if you want them.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes. RTC is on the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android. Tap a button above and your store opens.

How do I get the 14 days of Pro free?

Use the launch code RTCLAUNCH when you subscribe to RTC Pro in your store. It gives you 14 days of Pro to try everything before you pay.

Is it medical or dietitian advice?

No. RTC gives general fitness, nutrition and wellbeing guidance. Check with a doctor before you start if you have a medical condition or injury, are pregnant, take regular medication, or have a history of disordered eating.

Where is my data kept?

Your progress photos stay privately on your phone. You can read the full details in the privacy policy linked in the footer.
