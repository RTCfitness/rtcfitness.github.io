Questions

Subscription FAQ

How do I pay?

Through the App Store on iPhone or Google Play on Android. RTC never sees your card details.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Open your App Store or Google Play subscriptions and cancel there. You keep Pro until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I use both an iPhone and an Android phone. Will Pro follow me?

Pro includes cloud sync across devices. Subscriptions are bought through each store, so check your account when you switch.

What happens if I stop?

You drop back to the free plan. Your history stays, and the free tools keep working.

What does the RTCLAUNCH code do?

It gives you 14 days of RTC Pro free. You redeem it in the App Store or Google Play when you subscribe.
